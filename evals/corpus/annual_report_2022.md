# Annual Report 2022 - Arcturus Technologies Inc.

## Executive Summary

Arcturus Technologies Inc. reported total revenue of **274 million dollars** for the fiscal year ending December 31, 2022. Operating expenses totalled **181 million dollars**, yielding an operating profit of **93 million dollars**. Net income was **71 million dollars**.

The company employed **3,800 full-time employees** at year end.

## Revenue Breakdown

### Product Revenue

Product revenue was **197 million dollars**, representing 71.9 percent of total revenue. CloudSync Pro remained the lead product, contributing 121 million dollars.

### Services Revenue

Services revenue was **77 million dollars**, or 28.1 percent of total revenue.

## Operating Segments

### North America

North America generated 164.4 million dollars, or 60 percent of total revenue.

### Europe

Europe contributed 82 million dollars, or 29.9 percent of total revenue.

### Asia-Pacific

Asia-Pacific recorded 22.1 million dollars, representing growth of 28 percent year-over-year from 17.3 million dollars in 2021.

## Research and Development

R&D expenditure was **38 million dollars**, equivalent to 13.9 percent of revenue. The R&D headcount was **510 engineers**.

## Risk Factors

### Competition

Key competitors remain DataStream Corp and NexusCloud Ltd. The company launched its partner-certification programme in 2022 to strengthen its channel ecosystem.

## Outlook

Management projected revenue growth of 12 to 16 percent for 2023, contingent on successful execution of the CloudSync Pro version 4.0 release planned for Q1 2023.
