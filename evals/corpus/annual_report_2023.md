# Annual Report 2023 - Arcturus Technologies Inc.

## Executive Summary

Arcturus Technologies Inc. reported total revenue of **312 million dollars** for the fiscal year ending December 31, 2023, compared to 274 million dollars in 2022, representing a year-over-year growth of approximately **13.9 percent**.

Operating expenses for 2023 totalled **198 million dollars**, resulting in an operating profit of **114 million dollars**. Net income after taxes was **87 million dollars**, with an effective tax rate of 23.7 percent.

The company employed **4,200 full-time employees** worldwide at the end of 2023, up from 3,800 in 2022.

## Revenue Breakdown

### Product Revenue

Product revenue accounted for **72 percent of total revenue**, amounting to 224.6 million dollars. The flagship product, **CloudSync Pro**, generated 145 million dollars in product revenue, making it the single largest revenue contributor.

### Services Revenue

Services revenue, including professional services, implementation, and maintenance contracts, contributed **28 percent of total revenue**, or 87.4 million dollars.

## Operating Segments

### North America

The North America segment generated 187 million dollars in revenue, representing 60 percent of global revenue. Operating margin for this segment was 38 percent.

### Europe

The Europe segment contributed 93.6 million dollars, or 30 percent of global revenue. Operating margin was 31 percent, impacted by currency headwinds of approximately 4 million dollars.

### Asia-Pacific

The Asia-Pacific segment recorded 31.4 million dollars in revenue, or 10 percent of global revenue. This segment grew 42 percent year-over-year, the fastest of the three segments.

## Research and Development

Arcturus invested **47 million dollars** in research and development in 2023, equivalent to 15 percent of total revenue. The R&D team, comprising **620 engineers**, focused primarily on the next-generation CloudSync platform and artificial intelligence integrations.

## Risk Factors

### Competition

The enterprise software market is highly competitive. Key competitors include DataStream Corp, NexusCloud Ltd, and several open-source alternatives. Arcturus believes its primary competitive advantage is the depth of integration between CloudSync Pro and customers' existing infrastructure.

### Regulatory

The company is subject to data-protection regulations in all markets where it operates, including GDPR in Europe and CCPA in California. Compliance costs increased by 3.2 million dollars in 2023.

## Outlook

Management expects revenue growth of 10 to 14 percent in 2024, driven by continued expansion in the Asia-Pacific region and the planned launch of CloudSync Enterprise in the second quarter of 2024.
