# Data Governance Policy v1 - Arcturus Technologies Inc.

**Effective date**: January 1, 2022
**Document status**: Superseded by Policy v2 (effective January 1, 2024)

## 1. Purpose

This policy establishes minimum standards for the collection, storage, processing, and disposal of data assets at Arcturus Technologies Inc. It applies to all employees, contractors, and third-party service providers who access company or customer data.

## 2. Data Classification

All data handled by Arcturus is classified into one of four tiers:

| Tier | Label | Description |
|------|-------|-------------|
| 1 | Public | Information approved for public disclosure |
| 2 | Internal | General internal business information |
| 3 | Confidential | Sensitive business and customer data |
| 4 | Restricted | Highly sensitive data subject to regulatory controls |

## 3. Retention Schedule

Data must be retained according to the following schedule:

- **Tier 1 (Public)**: No minimum retention; may be deleted at any time.
- **Tier 2 (Internal)**: Retain for a minimum of **3 years**.
- **Tier 3 (Confidential)**: Retain for a minimum of **7 years**.
- **Tier 4 (Restricted)**: Retain for a minimum of **10 years** or as required by applicable law, whichever is longer.

## 4. Access Controls

Access to Tier 3 and Tier 4 data requires explicit manager approval and must be reviewed quarterly. Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for all systems handling Tier 3 or higher data.

## 5. Breach Notification

In the event of a confirmed data breach affecting Tier 3 or Tier 4 data, the Security team must notify affected customers within **72 hours** of confirmation. Regulatory notification must be made within the timeframes prescribed by applicable law.

## 6. Exceptions

Requests for exceptions to this policy must be submitted to the Chief Information Security Officer (CISO) and approved in writing before any deviation is permitted.

## 7. Review Cycle

This policy is reviewed annually. The next scheduled review is January 2023.
