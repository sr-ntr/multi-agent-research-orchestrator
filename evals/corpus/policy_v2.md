# Data Governance Policy v2 - Arcturus Technologies Inc.

**Effective date**: January 1, 2024
**Replaces**: Data Governance Policy v1 (effective January 1, 2022)

## 1. Purpose

This policy supersedes the v1 Data Governance Policy. It establishes updated standards for data management at Arcturus Technologies Inc., incorporating lessons from the 2023 GDPR compliance review and updated industry guidance on data minimisation.

## 2. Data Classification

The four-tier classification structure is retained from v1 with one change: Tier 2 is renamed from "Internal" to "Business Use" to clarify its scope.

| Tier | Label | Description |
|------|-------|-------------|
| 1 | Public | Information approved for public disclosure |
| 2 | Business Use | General internal business information (renamed from "Internal") |
| 3 | Confidential | Sensitive business and customer data |
| 4 | Restricted | Highly sensitive data subject to regulatory controls |

## 3. Retention Schedule

The retention periods have been updated from v1:

- **Tier 1 (Public)**: No minimum retention; may be deleted at any time.
- **Tier 2 (Business Use)**: Retain for a minimum of **2 years** (reduced from 3 years in v1).
- **Tier 3 (Confidential)**: Retain for a minimum of **7 years** (unchanged from v1).
- **Tier 4 (Restricted)**: Retain for a minimum of **10 years** (unchanged from v1).

## 4. Access Controls

Access controls from v1 are retained. New in v2: Tier 2 data now also requires multi-factor authentication when accessed remotely outside the corporate network.

## 5. Breach Notification

The 72-hour customer notification window is retained. New in v2: the Security team must file an internal incident report within **24 hours** of detecting a potential breach, regardless of confirmation status. This enables earlier containment.

## 6. Data Minimisation

New section in v2: The company must conduct annual data-minimisation reviews for all Tier 3 and Tier 4 datasets. Datasets with no documented business purpose must be deleted or downgraded within 90 days of the review.

## 7. Exceptions

Exception process unchanged from v1: written approval from the CISO is required.

## 8. Review Cycle

This policy is reviewed annually. The next scheduled review is January 2025.
