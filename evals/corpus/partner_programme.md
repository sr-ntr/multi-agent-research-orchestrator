# Partner Programme Guide - Arcturus Technologies Inc.

## Overview

The Arcturus Partner Programme enables third-party organisations (partners) to resell, implement, and build on top of CloudSync Pro. The programme was launched in **2022** as part of the company's channel expansion strategy.

## Partner Tiers

Partners are classified into three tiers based on annual certified sales volume and certification requirements:

| Tier | Annual Sales | Required Certifications | Discount on List Price |
|------|-------------|------------------------|------------------------|
| Registered | Any | 0 | 5% |
| Silver | > 250,000 USD | 2 | 15% |
| Gold | > 1,000,000 USD | 5 | 25% |

## Certification Exams

As of 2023, Arcturus offers **five certification tracks**:

1. **CloudSync Fundamentals** (CSF) -- required for Registered tier and above
2. **Implementation Specialist** (IS) -- covers deployment and configuration
3. **Security and Compliance** (SC) -- covers GDPR, CCPA, and data governance
4. **AI Automation Engineer** (AAE) -- covers the v4.0 AI Automation Engine feature
5. **Enterprise Architecture** (EA) -- covers large-scale deployment design

Each exam costs **200 dollars** per attempt. Certification is valid for **two years**. Partners may subsidise exam costs for their employees; Arcturus does not reimburse exam fees.

## Partner Benefits

All partners receive:
- Access to the partner portal at partner.arcturus.com
- A dedicated partner account manager (Silver and Gold only)
- Co-marketing opportunities (Gold only)
- Early access to product roadmap (Silver and Gold only)
- 24/7 priority support (Gold only; Silver receives business-hours support)

## Registration

New partners must complete the registration form at partner.arcturus.com and agree to the Partner Programme Agreement. Review and approval typically takes **10 business days**.

## Programme Statistics (2023)

At the end of 2023, the partner programme comprised:
- **312 Registered partners**
- **87 Silver partners**
- **24 Gold partners**

Total partner-sourced revenue in 2023 was approximately **48 million dollars**, representing about 15.4 percent of total company revenue.
